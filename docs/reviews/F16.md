## Checked and clean

  threshold, or can take anything away (empty-state copy, door label and hint, *"N finished
  pomodoros"*, *"Closes the garden."*). Finding 2 is the one way the screen does lose something.
- **The F12 fix is used correctly.** The sheet is presented from `TimerView`, which sits inside
  `ThemedRoot` (`ZenTomatoApp.swift:250`), so both the environment and the scene trait reach it.
  `theGardenGrowsTheThemesTomato` mirrors that with `.appTheme(.ripen)` on the presenting root and
  removes the scene trait in `defer`. Previews use `.environment(\.theme, …)` only — previews.
- **Large counts and AX sizes render.** Rendered at 1,044 / 13,000 / 50,000 poms, at `.large` and
  `.accessibility5`: no crash, the tomato colour present in every frame. The unbounded last row at
  50,000 is 187 items; not a practical concern at any real history.

  `GardenBadDayTests` together, 4 runs, 4/4 passed.
- **Todoist, secrets, licence, scope.** No Todoist call, no credential, no licence wording in the diff;
  nothing outside `F16`'s plan. `make check-todoist`, `check-secrets`, `check-licence`,
  `check-register` all exit 0.
