# C19 — the alarm that could not be silenced

**Owner: the agent.** Diagnosis only. No fix is proposed here, because the
mechanism has not been established and **this is the failure mode that has cost
this project the most**: the `F2b` alarm arc ran to four successive fixes, each
introduced by the one before it, every one of them a reasonable guess made
without a reproduction.

## What was reported

Build `202608281026`, 2026-08-28, at the end of a sprint:

> in the final short break, i tried clicking stop and instead of stopping, the
> sound continued and there was no way to stop the sound. I think the stop button
> has to be visible until clearly clicked on.

Everything else in that sprint passed, including both bells and the alarm firing
through Do Not Disturb.

## The one thing that is certain

**Nothing in this app can silence a ringing alarm.** `cancelAlarm()` is reachable
from exactly two engine paths — `stop(reason:)`, *after* a reason has been written
and confirmed, and one internal path — and **no view anywhere observes
`Alarm.State.alerting`**. The only control that ends a ringing alarm is the
Dismiss button on the alert iOS itself draws, backed by `DismissBlockIntent`.

That is true regardless of which mechanism below fired, and it is enough on its
own to produce "there was no way to stop the sound".

## Two mechanisms, not separated

**A — the button was not there any more.** `TimerView.swift:498`:

```swift
controls: engine.isRunning ? .running : .start(…)
```

`Stop` and `Start` are the same control in the same position, swapped on
`isRunning`. When a block ends, `isRunning` goes false and Stop becomes Start
**at the same instant the alarm begins to ring**. A tap aimed at Stop lands on
Start, or lands during the swap. This is what the owner's own diagnosis describes
— *"the stop button has to be visible until clearly clicked on"* — and it fits
"instead of stopping".

**B — Stop worked, and stopping does not silence anything.** `TimerView.swift:863`:
tapping Stop only opens `StopReasonSheet`. By design the block keeps running until
a reason is written and confirmed — *"Nothing here can end a block silently."*
While that sheet is up, an alarm that is already ringing keeps ringing, music
keeps playing, and the sheet covers the screen. Swiping it away confirms nothing.
This also fits, and it fits "no way to stop the sound" better than A does.

**They are not the same bug and they do not have the same fix.** A is a control
that moves under a finger. B is a modal that offers no way to silence what is
audible behind it.

## What would separate them

One question to the owner, and it is a question about what was on screen rather
than about what the app did:

- **Was the timer still counting down when Stop was tapped, or had the block
  already ended?** Still counting ⇒ B. Already ended ⇒ A.
- **Did a sheet asking "why are you stopping?" appear?** Appeared ⇒ B. Never
  appeared ⇒ A.

If neither is remembered — reasonable, it was one moment in a break — the
alternative is instrumentation: record `Alarm.State` transitions and control taps
to the existing diagnostics, and wait for the second occurrence.

## What is *not* proposed

**No fix, and specifically not "keep Stop visible".** Pinning Stop through the
boundary would mean a control labelled Stop that ends a block which has already
ended, next to an alarm it does not silence — plausible, cheap, and quite possibly
the fifth fix in a row that introduces the sixth.

The gap worth naming is the one that is certain: **while an alarm is alerting,
this app shows nothing that ends it.** Whatever is built should start there, and
it owes a delta because it is a new control on the timer screen.
