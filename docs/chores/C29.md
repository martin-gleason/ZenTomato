# C29 — the register lost 15 rows' text to a truncating extractor

**Kind:** chore. No user-visible change.
**Owner:** agent.
**Opened:** 2026-09-15, while starting `O44`.
**Branch:** `C29/the-register-is-lossy`

---

## What happened

`O44` said *"`docs/reviews/OPEN.md` is five items behind this register"* and proposed generating
`OPEN.md` from the register, the way `C28` had just done for `00-status.md`.

**Before generating anything, I checked whether the register actually held what `OPEN.md` held.** It
does not, and the loss runs in both directions.

### Direction one — the register was truncated

**15 of 43 register rows had their `Why` cut mid-word at about 110 characters**, by the upstream
`scripts/extract_zenpom.py` that its own header credits. `O1` ended *"The golden file is a for"*.

| Row | Was | Is | Lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| `O33` | 110 | 1266 | **91%** |
| `O30` | 110 | 773 | 86% |
| `O32` | 110 | 658 | 83% |
| `O35` | 110 | 640 | 83% |
| `O29` | 110 | 487 | 77% |
| `O25` | 110 | 473 | 77% |
| …and nine more | | | |

Repaired from `OPEN.md`, which held the complete text.

### Direction two — and this is why `O44` must not be actioned as written

**16 of `OPEN.md`'s 35 `O` ids are absent from the register entirely** — `O2`, `O6`, `O7`, `O10`,
`O11`, `O13`, `O14`, `O16`, `O17`, `O18`, `O19`, `O21`, `O22`, `O26`, `O27`, `O28`. All closed, and
each carries the reasoning for *how* it closed, which is the part worth keeping.

**Plus 83 lines of prose the register has no column for.** One of those paragraphs records that a laggy
button inflated the distraction tally, that the affected rows are still in the database, and that —
because there is no capture surface and no editor, by design — **nothing in the app can remove them, so
any export covering that block over-reports.** That is a caveat on `O1`, and it exists in no other file.

**So generating `OPEN.md` from the register would have destroyed 16 rows and 83 lines**, including a
warning about the integrity of the very export `O1` exists to judge. `O44`'s premise was wrong and its
proposed fix was worse than the problem.

---

## The bug in the first repair, kept because it is the interesting part

The first pass matched register rows to `OPEN.md` rows **by ID**. It reported 15 repairs and one of
them was wrong: the register's `O23` — *"Progress bars appear full rather than filling"* — received the
text of `~~O23~~` *"The two CC0 bell files, supplied and trimmed"*, a struck row about audio.

**`OPEN.md` carries two `O23`s, two `O24`s and two `O25`s.** The register already flags these collisions
as a defect in its own `## Defect — reused IDs` section. I read that section, filed `O43` about an
identical collision in `F8`'s mutation IDs earlier the same session, and then wrote a script that
assumed an ID identifies a row.

**A shared ID is not identity.** The repair now matches on **title**, and every one of the 15 landed at
a similarity of exactly 1.0 — so the match is not fuzzy in practice, it is exact, and the threshold
exists only to refuse a row it cannot confidently place. `O23` is now 118 characters of progress-bar
text rather than 627 characters about bells.

It was caught by spot-checking a repair against its source instead of trusting the count. The count was
15 both times.

---

## What was changed

- **15 register `Why` cells repaired** from `OPEN.md`, matched by title.
- **`O44` rewritten.** Its premise is corrected and the measurement is recorded in the row, with the
  decision it actually needs stated rather than assumed.
- **`C27` closed.** It had been closed once already and the edit was lost — reverted by a
  `git checkout -- docs/plans/00-register.md` that was cleaning up mutation `C28-M2` and discarded every
  uncommitted register change rather than the one the mutation had made. Recorded because the cleanup
  looked correct and was not.
- **`00-status.md` regenerated**, which `C28`'s gate now requires.

## What was deliberately not changed

**`OPEN.md` was not generated, not retired, and not edited.** Which of those happens is a decision about
where the narrative record lives, and it is the owner's. Both options are real:

- **Carry the 16 closed rows and the 83 prose lines into the register, then generate `OPEN.md`** — one
  source, at the cost of a migration that must not lose the prose, and a register that grows a column
  it does not currently have.
- **Keep `OPEN.md` as the narrative record and stop calling the register canonical for `O` rows** —
  cheaper, honest about what each file is for, and leaves two files to maintain.

`O44` stays open with the measurement attached, so whoever decides is deciding against numbers rather
than against the sentence that turned out to be wrong.
