## Why the app is not GPL

The GPL forbids adding restrictions beyond its own terms. App Store distribution
adds them — device limits and DRM apply to everyone who downloads. That conflict
is real and has removed apps before; VLC is the documented case.

MIT contains no such prohibition, so a binary distributed under it sits inside the
App Store's rules without contradiction. The copyright holder may license
different distributions on different terms, and does.
