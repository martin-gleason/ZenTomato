# App Store distribution exception

**One licence governs ZenPom — GPL-3.0-or-later, in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). This
file does not add a second one.** It records a pledge by the copyright holder
that resolves the one known conflict between that licence and Apple's App Store.

## The pledge

The ZenPom developers are aware that the terms of service that apply to apps
distributed via Apple's App Store may conflict with rights granted under
ZenPom's licence, the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option)
any later version. The copyright holder of ZenPom does not wish this conflict to
prevent the otherwise-compliant distribution of ZenPom or derived apps via the
App Store.

Therefore, the copyright holder commits not to pursue any licence violation that
results **solely** from the conflict between the GNU GPLv3 or any later version
and the Apple App Store terms of service. In other words: as long as you comply
with the GPL in all other respects — including its requirements to provide users
with the source code and the text of the licence — the copyright holder will not
object to your distribution of ZenPom, or of an app derived from it, through the
App Store.

## What this is, precisely

- **A non-enforcement pledge by the copyright holder**, in the form Nextcloud
  established for its iOS app and Signal's libsignal made an explicit additional
  permission. It is the pattern used by the GPL apps actually shipping on the App
  Store — Signal, Nextcloud, Telegram, Element, Bitwarden, Mastodon among them.
- **Scoped to exactly one conflict.** Every other obligation of the GPL stands:
  source availability, licence text, the copyleft on derived works. A fork that
  ships on the App Store still owes its users the source.
- **Binding on the copyright holder who made it**, and it survives for code they
  hold copyright in. Contributions are accepted only with the grant described in
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md), which is what keeps this pledge possible
  to give.

## What it is not

It is not a second licence, not a dual licence, and not a choice. There is
nothing to pick between: the GPL governs, and this file is the copyright holder
saying one specific enforcement will never come.
